What's inside

Everything a small shop needs to buy in bulk.

Sample first

Order a single unit at retail price before committing to a bulk order. One sample per account keeps it fair.

Co-Buy

Can't hit the minimum alone? Share an invite link and pool orders with other shops to reach the bulk price together.

Escrow protection

Your money is held safely until you confirm delivery. If something goes wrong, open a dispute with video evidence.

KHQR & card payments

Pay with any Bakong KHQR banking app or with Visa and Mastercard, processed by ABA PayWay.

Chat with suppliers

Negotiate and ask questions in the app. BosDom warns you about off-platform deals so you stay protected.

Local delivery

Shipping estimates from real Cambodian couriers, with parcel photos and delivery proof on every order.

How it works

From first sample to full shelf.

  1. 01

    Browse & sample

    Find suppliers by category, compare bulk price tiers, and order a sample to check quality.

  2. 02

    Order or Co-Buy

    Buy in bulk on your own, or join a Co-Buy with other shops to reach the volume price.

  3. 03

    Pay safely, receive

    Pay by KHQR or card. The supplier is paid only after you confirm your goods arrived.

Install on Android

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download the APK using the button on this page.
  2. Open the file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs if Android asks — tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then open BosDom and create your account.

This is an early test build, installed directly rather than from the Play Store. Payments currently run in PayWay's sandbox, so no real money moves.
